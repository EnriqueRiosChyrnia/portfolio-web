04Operational backoffice

Queues, roles & auditing

The problem. A platform with users and professionals generates internal work: records and documents someone has to review, a service catalog to maintain, and operational parameters to adjust without depending on a deploy.

My contribution. The backend that supports that operation. I designed a queue model with temporary leases so multiple reviewers can process documents in parallel without stepping on each other: each one claims a batch, and anything not completed in time goes back to the queue without getting lost. I implemented role-based access control with an identity plane separate from end users, an admin-configurable catalog and settings from the panel, and change auditing (who changed what, with before/after values). The panel's interface was built by someone else on the team; my work was the API and the rules behind it.

Conceptual model: reviewers work in parallel without collisions and no item gets lost; the system scales by adding reviewers.
